Closest taxonomic common ancestor: Negarnaviricota

Summary
- We compiled 40 target viruses and retrieved conserved polymerase proteins from NCBI/UniProt into proteins_raw/ according to the scheme:
  * Orthomyxoviruses (Influenza/Thogoto/Dhori/Quaranfil/Isavirus): PA and PB1
  * Others (Bunyaviruses/Hantaviruses/Arenaviruses/Rhabdo/Paramyxo/Filoviruses): L
- We built a metadata table with NCBI taxonomy ranks, accessions, sequence lengths: virus_metadata.csv
- Endonuclease-domain phylogeny (endonuclease_domains.*) was constructed from the N-terminal cap-snatching endonuclease regions (heuristically 1–240 aa with H–PD–D/E–K motif) from Orthomyxoviridae, Bunyavirales, and Arenaviridae representatives.
- Polymerase palm-domain phylogeny (polymerase_palm_domains.*) was constructed by extracting windows around the GDD catalytic motif from L/PB1.
- Multiple sequence alignment: MAFFT
- Trimming: gap-fraction filter (<=50% gaps per column)
- Tree inference: Neighbor-Joining (BLOSUM62) via Biopython (IQ-TREE not available server-side).

Key findings
- NCBI Taxonomy places Andes orthohantavirus (Bunyavirales: Hantaviridae) and Influenza A virus (Articulavirales: Orthomyxoviridae) under the same phylum Negarnaviricota but different classes (Bunyaviricetes vs Insthoviricetes).
- Endonuclease-domain tree shows Orthomyxovirus PA endonucleases grouping apart from Bunyavirales/Arenaviridae L endonucleases, joining at a deep node—consistent with an ancient common ancestor of segmented negative-sense RNA viruses (Polyploviricotina) with cap-snatching machinery.
- Polymerase palm-domain tree (limited taxon recovery around GDD motif) also separates non-segmented Mononegavirales from segmented groups.

Conclusion
- Closest taxonomic common ancestor: Negarnaviricota.
- Closest functional/domain ancestor: an ancestral segmented negative-sense RNA virus bearing cap-snatching endonuclease and RdRp palm catalytic machinery, preceding the divergence of Orthomyxoviridae and Bunyavirales.
