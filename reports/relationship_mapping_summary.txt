Interpretation
- The two endonucleases share a similar local pharmacophore kernel (2×ACC + 1×HPH) with close spatial agreement (RMSD ~1.85 Å), supporting analogous functional constraints at the pocket.
- The influenza PA pocket includes a metal cluster with acidic coordination; the Andes pocket shows multiple donor/positive features and no metal detected in the prepared structure. This indicates different chemical implementations achieving related functional roles (consistent with convergence at the active-site pharmacophore), but homology is not excluded by current data.
