Metal-aware redocking findings (ANDV L endonuclease, 6Q99 with aligned metal)
Date: 2026-05-12

What was done
1) Metal placement
- A divalent metal ion from the influenza PA pocket (6FS6) was aligned into the ANDV pocket (6Q99) via local CA-based Kabsch alignment around the pocket centers (10 Å spheres).
- Output receptor: receptors/6Q99/output_metal.pdb.

2) Ligand sets prepared
- Parents (safe): potential drugs/active_subset_safe.sdf (13 entries from active_subset_safe.csv).
- Modified analogues (safe): potential drugs/active_subset_modified_safe.sdf (155 entries, derived via F_scan/Me_scan/N_walk, then filtered for alerts/developability/toxicity heuristics).

3) Docking protocol
- App: playmolecule.apps.acedock (free mode), radius 8 Å around pocket1 template, 10 poses per ligand, pharmacophore rescoring disabled, using the metal-augmented receptor.
- Jobs:
  • docking/ANDV_6Q99_pocket1_metal_active (parents) – COMPLETED.
  • docking/ANDV_6Q99_pocket1_metal_modified (modified analogues) – COMPLETED.

Interpretation
- The ANDV pocket supports cross-targetability for certain chemotypes, but metal introduction without explicit coordination restraints causes a mixed outcome across the parent set.
- Modified analogues (especially N_walk and selected Me/F scans) show strong metal-aware docking scores, consistent with improved engagement near the catalytic site and with the shared 2×ACC + 1×HPH kernel observed in pharmacophore comparisons.

Safety context
- All docked ligands here were pre-filtered by structural alerts (PAINS/Brenk/NIH), property window (MW/cLogP/TPSA/etc.), and basic toxicity heuristics (hERG/electrophile/redox/mutagenicity proxies). See files in the 'potential drugs' folder, including toxicity_summary.txt.

Recommendations
1) Metal-coordination restraints docking: Re-run top candidates in acedock 'restraints' mode with distance restraints that enforce plausible metal–chelator geometry (e.g., 2.0–2.4 Å for O−Mn contacts; coordination numbers consistent with the scaffold). This should reduce artifacts and favor realistic poses for chelating series.
2) Pose inspection: Visualize the top 10 modified hits and the improved parents (e.g., CHEMBL4460007, CHEMBL4469214) in the 3D viewer to confirm sensible metal contacts and lack of steric/electrostatic clashes.
3) Physics refinement: Perform short MD relaxation of receptor + best docked ligands with explicit metal/waters, followed by MM-GBSA or more rigorous FEP for a small finalist set.
4) Safety follow-up: Apply QSAR models (hERG/CYP/Ames) to the short list; deprioritize any with elevated risk and iterate chemotype tuning (reduce cationic amphiphile character, modulate pKa/logP, limit polyaromatic rings).
5) Experimental plan: Progress 5–10 top-ranked, toxicity-clean modified analogues to biochemical endonuclease assays; include a few improved parents as references for SAR linkage.
